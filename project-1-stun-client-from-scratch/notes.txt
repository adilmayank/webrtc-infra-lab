Now any other client/server on the other side of this router/nat will not see the request coming from my local IP, because the Router/NAT changes the source of the packets and make it as if router/nat itself is the source of those packets. Similarly in the case of STUN server, it sees the packets coming from the public IP of my own router/nat and not my private device IP. Its the responsbility of router to maintain the internal mapping of devices and connections because 2 devices with 192.168.1.10 local ip can have two individual request like 192.168.1.10:52341 and 192.168.1.10:62342, so the router rewrites them to 49.205.176.7:52341 and 49.205.176.7:62342 but maintains an internal mapping so that packets with destination 49.205.176.7:62342 reaches 192.168.1.10:62342.
